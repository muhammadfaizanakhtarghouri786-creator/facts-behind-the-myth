Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Facts Behind the Myth Uploader (“the app”), a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Facts Behind the Myth (@FactsBehindTheMyth) to manage that channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app uses these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's own videos;
  • youtube: to set the channel's own video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists, schedule, banner and watermark;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add subtitle (caption) files to the channel's own videos.

The app reads only the information needed for those tasks, such as the channel's ID and the status of its uploads. It does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, is never shared, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app has no servers, databases or analytics, and it does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the contact details on the channel's YouTube About page.
