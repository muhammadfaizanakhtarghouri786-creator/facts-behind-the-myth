Facts Behind the Myth

Strange stories. Real explanations.

Facts Behind the Myth is a YouTube channel that takes myths, mysteries and strange-but-true stories and shows the real history and science behind them. Every video lists its sources.

Watch on YouTube → @FactsBehindTheMyth

About the Facts Behind the Myth Uploader

The channel uses a small private tool, the Facts Behind the Myth Uploader, to upload its own videos, thumbnails and video details to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.
